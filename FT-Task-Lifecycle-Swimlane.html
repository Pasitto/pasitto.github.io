State models

FT Task (driver-created)

Accepted→Arriving→On Delivery→CompletedPartially CompletedReturned
Assigning skipped (creator = assignee). Drops added while On Delivery. Cancelled only before pickup.

Order (drop)

Created→Delivering→DeliveredFailed
Created on-site by FT Driver or 3PL Driver (synced).

3PL Replacement Task

Assigning→Accepted→Arriving→On Delivery→CompletedCancelledFailed
Mirrors SKOOTAR Job status. Cancelled/Failed → flagged to SKL Ops.

Replacement Request (FTA)

Draft→Submitted→Created→ClosedWithdrawn
Submitted ⇄ ApiFailed (retry, same idempotency key).

Worklog row (FTA)

Pending Verify→VerifiedFlagged
Row types: FT_DRIVER_DAY, 3PL_REPLACEMENT. Source change re-opens to Pending Verify. Verified ≠ billing approval.

Missed Clock-in alert (FTA)

Open→Acknowledged→Resolved – clocked inResolved – replacedDismissed
Grace 5 min · check every 15 min (Tech Lead may shorten) · alert guaranteed ≤ 20 min after shift start.
